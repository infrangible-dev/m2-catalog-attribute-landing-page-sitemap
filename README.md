# Infrangible Catalog Attribute Landing Page Sitemap

Adds the landing pages and groups of
[Infrangible Catalog Attribute Landing Page](https://www.infrangible.dev/modules/catalog-attribute-landing-page) to the
XML sitemaps: to the sitemap of Magento and to the sitemap of
[Infrangible Sitemap](https://www.infrangible.dev/modules/sitemap).

## Sitemaps

The sitemap of a store contains the active landing pages and groups of the store view (and of all store views) that
are not excluded, with their URL in the store (the URL key with the URL suffix of categories of the store) and the date
of their last change.

## Configuration

- Stores > Configuration > Catalog > XML Sitemap > Attribute Landing Page (sitemap of Magento) and
- Stores > Configuration > Infrangible Solutions > Sitemap > Attribute Landing Page (Infrangible Sitemap):

  Page and Group: export, change frequency and priority. By default, pages and groups are exported with the change
  frequency "monthly" and the priority 1.0.

The forms of the pages and groups (Infrangible > Attribute Landing Pages) have the field "Exclude from Sitemap".

## Requirements

- Magento Open Source or Adobe Commerce 2.4.6 or newer
- PHP 8.1 or newer
